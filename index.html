Brandi's Fantasy Assistant

Brandi's Fantasy Assistant is a private, personal tool for my own use in two Yahoo Fantasy Football leagues. It is not a product, is not available to anyone else, and is not sold or monetized.

What it does

It runs only on my personal computer and lets my AI assistant, Claude (made by Anthropic), read my Yahoo Fantasy league data when I ask a question, such as who to start, which available players are worth adding from the waiver wire, or how a trade would affect my team. It also runs a few scheduled checks, such as my starters' injury status before games. It has read-only access and cannot change anything in my leagues.

Privacy

The tool signs in through Yahoo's official login (OAuth) with my own Yahoo account, and its credentials are stored encrypted on my computer. League data is requested fresh each time and is not stored. Yahoo user IDs are removed before any data reaches Claude. League data, including other members' team names, is shown only to me in my private Claude chats, where Anthropic's privacy policy applies, and is never published or shared. Yahoo collects and uses data under its own privacy policy.

Fantasy data provided by Yahoo Fantasy.
